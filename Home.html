Welcome to Lesotho Tourism Website

Lesotho is a beautiful mountainous country in Southern Africa. It is often called mountain kingdom because of its high-altitude landscape. Tourism is supported by the countrys natural beauty, Basotho traditional culture and mountain adventure. Best time to visit, Autumn (March to May) and Spring (September to November) offers the most pleasant weather for hiking and exploring.

  • Geography and climate
  • Mountains and highlands
  • Basotho tradition
  • Language and currency
  • traditional clothing and food
